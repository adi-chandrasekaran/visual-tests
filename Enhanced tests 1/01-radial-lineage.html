Reading the atlas

The centre is the common ancestor. Splits mark ancestral branching. Outer labels identify observed clones; dotted extensions align their annotations. Rings describe each terminal clone, not its ancestors.

Placeholder topology, branch lengths, states, variants, and dates are invented. No biological inference or calibration is implied.

Keyboard: Tab to nodes; Enter or Space to inspect.
